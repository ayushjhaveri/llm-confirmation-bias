Triples that fit this rule are called DAX, and triples that do not fit this rule are called MED.

These rules are concerned with the common attributes or relations between any three numbers,
and not with their absolute magnitude - for example, they are not rules like "all numbers above (or below) 50."

You will then be told if the triple conforms with the DAX rule or the MED rule.
